One workspace, not three apps

Instead of juggling a to-do list, a clipboard manager, and a time tracker, Trakisilo keeps them in the same project context — plan, do, and review without losing the thread.

Kanban board & to-do list

Tasks and subtasks with rich descriptions, comments, attachments, labels, estimates, and due dates. Colored cards, card links, custom statuses, and bulk paste-add.

Clipboard manager & saved clips

Save reusable text, code, links, tables, and images — organize into panels and boards, quick-paste anywhere with a customizable shortcut. Explicit saves only — nothing is recorded in the background.

Time tracker & timesheet

Timers tied to real tasks or manual entries, overlap warnings, leave days, weekly timesheets, grouped reports, and CSV export.

Chronicle — activity journal

An automatic daily feed of what you did, with space for your own notes — plus rich project documents per board.

Rich documents

Code blocks with syntax highlighting, tables, collapsible toggles, callouts, and mermaid diagrams. Select text and turn it into anything.

Processes & calendar

Turn recurring work into reusable checklist templates and run them step by step. See tasks and logged time on a calendar.

Private by design

Offline-first. No account to create, no telemetry to opt out of — nothing leaves your machine unless you export it yourself. Local backup and restore keep you in control.
